Hi, I’m Xygarathma — an art director and designer who grew up among the vibrant landscapes of Malaysia.

For 10 years I’ve worked across web and print, branding, events and set design, with a degree in animation and a soft spot for 3D. My work has won a Symbol Award Gold and Best International Short Film, and appears in Apple stores worldwide.

Off the clock: jewellery making, hiking, gaming, and the weird and wonderful sides of life.
